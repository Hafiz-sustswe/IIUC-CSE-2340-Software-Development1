About the Department

The department offers a practical, project-based curriculum. Students build and deploy real applications from the third semester onwards, and every course is assessed through individual demonstration as well as written work.

  • 15 teaching weeks per semester
  • 6 individual mini projects in CSE-2340
  • 1 group showcase at the end of term
