About the Department

Scroll through this section to test the sticky header properly.

The department runs a practical, project-based curriculum.

Students build and deploy real applications from the third semester.

Every course is assessed by individual demonstration as well as written work.

Mini projects are evaluated in dedicated classes.

The final group project integrates React, routing and Firebase.

Teams are formed in Week 8.

Each member owns at least one feature end to end.
